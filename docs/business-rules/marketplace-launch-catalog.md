name: Marketplace launch catalog
description: Product types included in the first real-sales release.
---

The first real-sales release includes sunglasses and optical frames without prescription lenses. Prescription-lens customization and fulfillment are deferred.

**Why:** The owner chose a manageable first assortment; prescription products would need additional prescription checks, measurements, and preparation steps before reliable fulfillment.

**How to apply:** Keep launch product forms, search, checkout, and buyer-facing promises focused on ready-to-sell sunglasses and frames. Do not present prescription-lens selection or custom-made eyewear as an active checkout feature until explicitly added later.
