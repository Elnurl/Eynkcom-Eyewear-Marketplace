---
name: Marketplace guest checkout
description: Buyer account requirement for real purchases and follow-up access.
---

Buyers may place orders as guests; creating an account is optional. Guest status updates and return access must be designed without requiring registration.

**Why:** The owner chose an optional buyer account rather than making sign-up a prerequisite for purchase.

**How to apply:** Allow a guest to complete the same multi-shop checkout as an account holder. Protect guest order details and return actions with a secure, buyer-specific verification or access flow; a public order number alone must not grant access. Do not conflate the optional buyer account with the mandatory authenticated, shop-scoped seller access.
