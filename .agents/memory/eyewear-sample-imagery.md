---
name: Eyewear sample imagery
description: Visual quality constraint for generated eyewear product cutouts
---

Generated transparent eyewear packshots can still contain an opaque checkerboard inside clear lenses, even when the outer background has a valid alpha channel. Inspect the lenses on the actual white product card, not only the image's transparency metadata.

**Why:** A sample image looked like a clean cutout at first glance but displayed a checker pattern through its lenses in the storefront. This undermined the precise product-photo presentation the user requested.

**How to apply:** For future generated eyewear samples, verify clear lenses on white and clean any baked-in checker pattern before using the image. Keep mockup visuals clearly distinct from real seller-uploaded photos.
