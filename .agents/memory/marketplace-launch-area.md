name: Marketplace launch area
description: Geographic boundary for the initial real-order launch.
---

The first real-order launch should accept orders in Bakı and Abşeron only; nationwide ordering can be considered later.

**Why:** The owner chose a limited first sales area while shaping fulfillment and payment operations.

**How to apply:** Scope checkout addresses, delivery availability, and buyer-facing promises to Bakı and Abşeron for launch. Do not imply countrywide fulfillment until the owner expands the operating area.
